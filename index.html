Helpful information

Delivery & questions

Do you deliver across the UK?

Yes. Standard UK delivery is a flat £4.50 per order, whether you buy one item or several. Delivery is available within the UK only. We confirm the order and payment arrangements before you pay.

How do I order?

Add your favourites to your basket and select “Request an order”. Submit your enquiry and we'll confirm availability, delivery details and payment instructions. An enquiry is not a confirmed purchase.

Are all the items handmade?

Our plaster creations are handmade, and the range includes handmade gifts and craft items. Please refer to each product photograph and description for what's included.

Will my painting kit have the same paint colours as the photo?

Paint colours may vary from those shown in our product photographs. Each painting kit includes paint pots and a brush as described in its product listing.

Are painting kits suitable for very young children?

Paint sets and small parts may not suit very young children. Please check the product's safety information, age guidance and adult-supervision requirements before purchasing.

What about returns?

Returns and cancellation arrangements, including your statutory rights, will be supplied with our full seller details before you pay. Please contact the shop if anything arrives damaged.
